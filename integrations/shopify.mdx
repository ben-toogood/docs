---
title: Shopify
---

To connect your Shopify account to Pimento, follow the below steps:

1. Navigate to **Integrations** this can be found by going to Settings > Integrations on the Pimento dashboard.

<img height="200" src="/images/settings-integrations.png" alt="Integrations page" />

2. Next go to **Connect New Integration**

<img height="200" src="/images/connect-new-integration.png" alt="Connect new integration" />

3. From there you will be shown a pop-up asking to install from the Shopify App store, simply hit **Connect** and you will be redirected. If you see a slightly different screen please refer to 3.1 below

<img height="200" src="/images/shopify-brands.png" alt="Shopify brand click connect" />

3.1 If you see a slightly different screen you will be asked to enter your slug, this can be found when you go to `admin.shopify.com` within the domain bar, e.g from `https://admin.shopify.com/store/ethan-testing-store` you would need to enter `ethan-testing-store`

<img height="200" src="/images/shopify-3pl.png" alt="3PL enter store slug" />


4. You will now be shown the Shopify App Store, click Install, you will need to ensure you are logged into the correct Shopify store you wish to link.

<img height="200" src="/images/shopify-app-store.png" alt="Shopify App Store" />

4. When re-directed to Shopify you will be prompted to accept the Pimento App Shopify permissions, these are required to download and send fulfilment updates

<img height="200" src="/images/shopify-permissions.png" alt="Shopify Settings Page" />

5. Once accepted you will be redirected back to Pimento with your settings page now available, you will **NEED** to set a fulfilment location so that orders can then begin to download
<img height="200" src="/images/shopify-settings.png" alt="Pimento Shopify Settings page" />
